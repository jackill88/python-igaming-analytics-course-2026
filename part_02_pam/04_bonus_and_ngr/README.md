# Module 04: Bonuses and NGR

GGR from Chapter 1, minus the costs PAM can finally see.

## Learning Objectives

- Separate a bonus definition, a claim, and wagering progress
- Compute NGR from GGR, bonus cost, a provider-fee rate, and a tax rate, in EUR
- Measure how much of a player's stake — and therefore of GGR — sat on bonus balance
