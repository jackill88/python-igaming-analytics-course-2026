# Activity and Cohorts Quiz

8 multiple-choice questions. Answers at the bottom.

---

**1.** Login DAU counts players who:

- A) Settled a game round
- B) Have a login session that day
- C) Have any wallet transaction
- D) Registered that day

**2.** Which deposit status counts toward deposit DAU?

- A) approved
- B) open
- C) rejected
- D) Any status, if tx_type is deposit

**3.** A withdrawal on an otherwise quiet day:

- A) Increments deposit DAU
- B) Increments play DAU
- C) Does not increment deposit DAU
- D) Is the FTD

**4.** An FTD cohort excludes players who:

- A) Have no approved deposit
- B) Registered more than 7 days ago
- C) Played before they deposited
- D) Use EUR

**5.** Day-1 retention in this chapter means play on:

- A) The anchor calendar day
- B) The calendar day anchor_date + 1
- C) Any day in the next 7 days
- D) The registration month

**6.** Registration D1 and FTD D1 differ because:

- A) GGR uses a different formula
- B) The cohort membership and the anchor date change
- C) EUR conversion is optional
- D) Login sessions replace rounds

**7.** A self-excluded player with rounds should be:

- A) Deleted from GGR
- B) Kept in revenue facts and removed from the CRM population
- C) Given a higher churn score so CRM emails them
- D) Counted as a new registration

**8.** marketing_email_allowed = 0 means:

- A) The player is the FTD for the brand
- B) The player stays out of the marketing contact set
- C) Their deposits are void
- D) Their rounds move to another brand

---

## Answers

1. B · 2. A · 3. C · 4. A · 5. B · 6. B · 7. B · 8. B
